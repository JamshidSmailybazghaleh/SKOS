# Mission

Transform the SKOS ecosystem from an architecture-focused
project into an operational platform capable of generating
its first sustainable revenue.

---

# Primary Objective

Deliver the First Revenue as early as possible while preserving
the architectural quality established by the SAS standards.

---

# Success Criteria

The operation is considered successful when:

- The first digital product is released.
- The first customer acquires the product or service.
- The first revenue is recorded.
- The delivery process is repeatable.
