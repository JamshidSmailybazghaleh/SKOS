---

# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the governance framework for the
architecture of the Smaily Knowledge Operating System (SKOS).

Its purpose is to ensure that architectural decisions remain
consistent, documented, reviewable and sustainable throughout
the lifecycle of the project.

---

# 2. Scope

Applies to:

- Architecture
- Standards
- Kernel
- Engines
- Services
- Pipelines
- Connectors
- Schemas
- Documentation
- Repository

---

# 3. Governance Principles

Architecture shall be:

- Transparent
- Traceable
- Reviewable
- Versioned
- Evidence-based
- Backward-aware
- Sustainable

---

# 4. Governance Roles

Architecture Owner

Responsible for the long-term architecture.

Architecture Reviewer

Reviews proposals and compliance.

Component Owner

Responsible for individual modules.

Contributor

Implements approved changes.

---

# 5. Decision Categories

Major Decisions

Require architecture approval.

Minor Decisions

Require component review.

Editorial Changes

May be approved by component owners.

---

# 6. Architecture Decision Records

Every major decision shall create an ADR.

Minimum fields:

ADR ID

Title

Context

Decision

Alternatives

Consequences

Approval Date

Related Standards

---

# 7. Review Cycle

Architecture Review

Every 6 months

Standards Review

Every 12 months

Emergency Review

Whenever critical issues arise.

---

# 8. Compliance Review

Every component shall be reviewed against:

SAS-001

SAS-002

SAS-003

...

Applicable standards only.

---

# 9. Change Control

Every proposed change shall include:

Purpose

Impact

Compatibility

Migration

Risk

Approval

---

# 10. Exception Process

Exceptions shall:

Be documented

Have an expiration date

Include justification

Be reviewed

---

# 11. Governance Metrics

Recommended metrics:

Compliance Rate

Architecture Debt

Open ADRs

Standards Coverage

Review Completion Rate

---

# 12. Compliance

This governance process is mandatory for all official
SKOS architecture changes.
