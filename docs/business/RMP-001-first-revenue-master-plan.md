# Mission

Achieve the first verified revenue of the SKOS ecosystem
through a structured and measurable execution plan.

---

# Phase 1 — Product Selection

Objective:

Select ONE flagship product for immediate release.

Deliverables:

- Product selected
- Scope approved
- Publication schedule approved

Exit Criteria:

Product ready for production.

---

# Phase 2 — Product Completion

Objective:

Prepare the product for commercial publication.

Checklist:

- Final manuscript
- Technical review
- Editorial review
- Design
- Metadata
- Final quality review

Exit Criteria:

Publication package completed.

---

# Phase 3 — Market Preparation

Objective:

Prepare all commercial assets.

Deliverables:

- Landing page
- Product description
- Pricing
- Frequently Asked Questions
- Preview pages

Exit Criteria:

Sales channel ready.

---

# Phase 4 — Launch

Objective:

Release the product.

Checklist:

- Product published
- Payment verified
- Delivery verified
- Support ready

Exit Criteria:

First customer purchase.

---

# Phase 5 — Validation

Objective:

Confirm the business model.

Metrics:

- Visitors
- Conversion rate
- Sales
- Customer feedback
- Revenue

Exit Criteria:

First confirmed revenue received.

---

# Phase 6 — Scale

Objective:

Turn the launch process into a repeatable system.

Deliverables:

- Updated production workflow
- Lessons learned
- Improved templates
- Roadmap for additional products

---

# Success Definition

The operation succeeds when:

- The first product is sold.
- Revenue is received.
- The process is documented.
- The workflow is repeatable.
