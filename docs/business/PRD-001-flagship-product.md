# Mission

Define, develop and publish the first commercial knowledge
product of the SKOS ecosystem.

---

# Product Criteria

The first product shall:

- Deliver clear value.
- Be professionally edited.
- Be publishable within a short timeframe.
- Demonstrate the quality of SKOS.
- Be repeatable as a production model.
