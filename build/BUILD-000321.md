Build Title

Knowledge Graph Core

---

Objective

Design and implement the first operational Knowledge Graph Engine, enabling SKOS to represent knowledge as an interconnected graph rather than isolated files and folders.

This Build establishes the structural foundation for semantic navigation, intelligent reasoning, and autonomous knowledge discovery.

---

Mission

Transform the SKOS Repository into a living knowledge network where every Knowledge Object can be connected, explored, analysed, and interpreted through explicit relationships.

---

Vision

The Knowledge Graph will become the central intelligence layer of SKOS, serving as the bridge between stored knowledge and machine-assisted understanding.

---
